Last updated: October 2026

Arts Studios sells digital prompt packs delivered instantly via promptmaharaja.in. Please read this policy before purchasing.

1. Digital Products

Because our prompt packs are digital and accessible immediately after purchase, all sales are generally final and non-refundable.

2. Exceptions

We will consider a refund when:

  • You were charged twice for the same prompt pack.

3. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund under the exceptions above, email hello@promptmaharaja.in within 7 days of purchase with your order details. We will review and respond within 3–5 business days.

4. Chargebacks

Please contact us first before initiating a chargeback so we can resolve the issue directly.

5. Free Prompts

Free prompts carry no purchase and no refund obligation.

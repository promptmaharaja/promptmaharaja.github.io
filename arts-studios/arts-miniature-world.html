What is Miniature World?

Miniature World is a premium free image-to-image category of Arts Studios (promptmaharaja.in) with 250 full detailed copy-ready prompts across 10 subcategories — City Miniature, Village Miniature, Home Miniature, Festival Miniature, Wedding Miniature, Shop Miniature, Nature Miniature, Food Miniature, Office Miniature and Fantasy Miniature.

Upload your photo, paste a prompt, and watch the AI shrink your world into a tiny, adorable miniature scene — perfect for creative DPs, gifts and social media posts.

How to Use

  1. Pick a subcategory above — for example City Miniature or Wedding Miniature.
  2. Choose a prompt from the 25 prompts inside and tap Copy.
  3. Upload your photo in your favourite AI image tool (image-to-image mode).
  4. Paste the prompt, generate, and share your tiny miniature world.

All 250 prompts are premium free — no sign-up, no payment.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 | Website: promptmaharaja.in

At PromptMaharaja and ARTS Studios Tool (promptmaharaja.in), we respect your privacy. This policy explains how data is handled across our prompt library, blog pages, and AI Image Generator tool.

1. Information We Collect

  • No Account Required: You can browse all 3500+ prompts and use the AI Image Generator without registering or sharing your name or email.
  • AI Image Generator Prompts: When you generate an image in our ARTS Studios AI Image Generator, the text prompt you type is sent to the Pollinations.ai generation endpoint to create your image. Generated images in "This Session" remain temporarily in your browser memory and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Contact Enquiries: If you email us at hello@promptmaharaja.in, we receive your email address and message solely to reply to you.
  • Standard Technical Logs & Cookies: Browser type, device info, anonymized IP address, and pages visited on promptmaharaja.in.
Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosure:
We use Google AdSense to display advertisements across promptmaharaja.in. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For further details, review the Google Advertising Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and browser storage to remember tool preferences, measure traffic via Google Analytics, and display relevant AdSense ads. You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense — serves advertisements across promptmaharaja.in.
  • Google Analytics (G-0S31ZT26JL) — provides anonymized site traffic and usage insights.
  • Pollinations.ai — processes text-to-image generation requests made inside the ARTS Studios AI Image Generator tool.
  • GitHub Pages / Web Hosting — serves the static files of promptmaharaja.in securely over HTTPS.

4. Data Protection & Children's Privacy

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Our services are intended for users aged 13 and above.

1. What is Image-to-Image AI?

Unlike text-to-image prompts, image-to-image workflows require your reference photo. You upload your picture into an AI tool like Midjourney or Stable Diffusion, paste our specialized prompt, and let the AI transform it.

2. Frequently Asked Questions

  • Are Arts Studios prompts free? Yes, all 2500+ image-to-image prompts are completely free.
  • How do I keep my face likeness? Set your image weight parameter between 0.6 and 0.8 in your AI generator.

What is Name Poster Studio?

Name Poster Studio is a premium free image-to-image category of Arts Studios (promptmaharaja.in) with 250 full detailed copy-ready prompts across 10 subcategories — Stylish Name Poster, Mom Special, Dad Special, Couple Poster, Birthday Poster, Anniversary Poster, Baby Poster, Friendship Poster, Attitude Poster and Festival Poster.

Upload your photo, paste a prompt, and the AI transforms it into a beautiful personalised poster — perfect for WhatsApp DPs, birthday wishes, anniversary surprises and festival greetings.

How to Use

  1. Pick a subcategory above — for example Birthday Poster or Attitude Poster.
  2. Choose a prompt from the 25 prompts inside and tap Copy.
  3. Upload your photo in your favourite AI image tool (image-to-image mode).
  4. Paste the prompt, generate, and share your stunning name poster.

All 250 prompts are premium free — no sign-up, no payment.

👑 About PromptMaharaja

Last updated: October 2026

PromptMaharaja (promptmaharaja.in) is a free AI prompts library for creators, designers, and AI artists. The site offers 3500+ copy-ready sample prompts across 7 categories — every prompt is free to copy and use in your favourite AI image tools.

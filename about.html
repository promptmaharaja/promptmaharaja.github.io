About PromptMaharaja

PromptMaharaja is a prompt library created for creators, designers, and AI artists who want ready-to-use ideas without wasting time on weak or repetitive prompts.

Main library

PromptMaharaja

Our main library is focused on text-to-image prompts. It includes categories like AI portraits, cinematic scenes, anime, festivals, product ads, logos, and poster design. The goal is simple: helpful, copy-ready prompts that work as a starting point for your creative workflow.

Image-to-image

Arts Studios by SULTAN

Arts Studios is a separate creative hub for image-to-image transformation prompts. It is designed for users who upload a photo and transform it into a stylized version such as a poster, retro look, fantasy scene, fashion aesthetic, or mini world composition.

Our mission

We want to make AI creativity easier by organizing prompts into useful categories, so people can start making better results faster. The site is built around clarity, speed, and creative usefulness.

How to Reach Us

Right now email is our only official contact channel — this keeps every conversation in one place so nothing gets lost. Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Tap the Compose Email button above — it opens Gmail with our address hello@promptmaharaja.in already filled in.
  • Step 2: Write a clear subject line, for example "Refund request — order #1234" or "New style request: neon portraits".

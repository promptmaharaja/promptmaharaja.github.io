What is 3D Cartoon Studio?

3D Cartoon Studio is a premium free image-to-image category of Arts Studios (promptmaharaja.in) with 250 full detailed copy-ready prompts across 10 subcategories — Pixar Style, Anime Style, Chibi Style, Disney Style, Superhero Cartoon, Fantasy Cartoon, Funny Cartoon, Baby Cartoon, Pet Cartoon and Retro Cartoon.

Upload your photo, paste a prompt, and the AI transforms you into a fun 3D cartoon character — perfect for playful DPs, kids photos, and viral social media posts.

How to Use

  1. Pick a subcategory above — for example Pixar Style or Chibi Style.
  2. Choose a prompt from the 25 prompts inside and tap Copy.
  3. Upload your photo in your favourite AI image tool (image-to-image mode).
  4. Paste the prompt, generate, and share your 3D cartoon avatar.

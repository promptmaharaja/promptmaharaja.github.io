👑 About PromptMaharaja & ARTS Studios Tool

Last updated: November 2026 | Website: promptmaharaja.in

PromptMaharaja (promptmaharaja.in) is a creator-focused AI platform built in India. We combine a curated library of 3500+ full detailed copy-ready text-to-image prompts across 7 categories with our built-in ARTS Studios AI Image Generator Tool — allowing creators to either copy prompts for external platforms or generate AI images directly inside their browser with zero sign-up.
